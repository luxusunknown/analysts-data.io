Add a new recap

Paste the raw HTML (or plain text) of one or more new #recaps days below — the same kind of export you'd save from Discord. It's parsed entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

After merging, download data.json and commit/push it to your GitHub repo (overwriting the old one) to publish the update for everyone. See README.md for the full workflow.
