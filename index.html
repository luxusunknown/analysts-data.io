Copy-trade simulator

A simplified hypothetical, not a replay of real trades: each run risks the chosen % of your current balance on every call this analyst posted (compounding), applying that call's real % return to your risk amount instead of their actual dollar figures — we don't know their real position size, only their % move. It assumes you can always size in exact proportion to your risk budget; in reality options trade in whole contracts, and some calls cost more per contract than your entire risked amount, so a real account this size couldn't necessarily take every single call. Use this to compare analysts side by side, not as a literal "you would have made $X" prediction. Historical simulation only — not financial advice.
